Choose which products alert you, and hear about price drops.

**Already on 3.0 or 3.1?** The app offers this update by itself within a day, or now from **Settings → Updates → Check for updates**.

**New here?** Download [`UnifiStockWatcher-Setup.exe`](https://github.com/jamesccupps/UnifiStockWatcher/releases/download/v3.2.0/UnifiStockWatcher-Setup.exe) (installs for your Windows user, no admin prompt) or the portable [`UnifiStockWatcher-win64.zip`](https://github.com/jamesccupps/UnifiStockWatcher/releases/download/v3.2.0/UnifiStockWatcher-win64.zip). The app isn't code-signed, so Windows may say *Windows protected your PC*: **More info → Run anyway**.

### New

- **Mute one product.** Right-click a card (or its **⋯** menu) → **Mute alerts**. It's still checked and kept in the history, but doesn't alert. A crossed-out bell on the card shows it's muted; click the bell to unmute.
- **Only for favourites.** **Settings → Alerts → Only for favourites** alerts only for the products you've starred. The same switch under **Alerts on your phone** keeps the others off your phone, so they alert on the PC only.
- **Price changes.** The history now records every price change of a product you watch, as *Price down* or *Price up* with the old price, including changes that happened while the app was closed. Turn on **Settings → Alerts → Tell me when a price drops** for a notification (and a phone alert, if that's on) when one gets cheaper.

For anyone using *Any address (JSON)*: a price drop arrives with `"event": "price_drop"` and the old price in `product.price_was`, which is `null` in an in-stock alert.
