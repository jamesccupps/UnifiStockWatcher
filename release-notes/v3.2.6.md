Fixes from a code review. No new features.

**Already on 3.0 or later?** The app offers this update by itself within a day, or now from **Settings → Updates → Check for updates**. Installed with Scoop: `scoop update unifistockwatcher`.

**New here?** Download [`UnifiStockWatcher-Setup.exe`](https://github.com/jamesccupps/UnifiStockWatcher/releases/download/v3.2.6/UnifiStockWatcher-Setup.exe) (installs for your Windows user, no admin prompt) or the portable [`UnifiStockWatcher-win64.zip`](https://github.com/jamesccupps/UnifiStockWatcher/releases/download/v3.2.6/UnifiStockWatcher-win64.zip), or install it with [Scoop](https://scoop.sh):

```
scoop bucket add jamesccupps https://github.com/jamesccupps/scoop-bucket
scoop install jamesccupps/unifistockwatcher
```

If Windows shows *Windows protected your PC*, **More info** names the publisher, James Cupps; `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists every file's hash.

### Fixed

- **The daily update check could stop for good** in a copy left running: one that came due while a check or download was running, or while *Check for updates* was off, ended them until the next restart. Now it keeps its schedule, and turning the setting back on needs no restart.
- **Changing the store alerted for everything already in stock there.** Now a product that's in stock in the new store doesn't alert; its next return does, as after a restart.
- **One product with an odd title or price in the store's data could break *Add products***, or the command-line watcher's first run. They now list it by its name, or its product id when it has none.
- **A product page in an unexpected shape was retried three times, with pauses, every check.** It's now answered at once.
- **If the watch list couldn't be saved** after a star, a mute, an add or a remove, the activity log says so (the change applies until the app closes), instead of the generic error dialog.
- **Phone alerts still being sent when you quit** (or when the app restarts for an update, or the command-line watcher is stopped) get up to 3 seconds to go out instead of being dropped.
- A quick check started just before a store change no longer marks the product delisted in the new store.
- The History filter waits for you to stop typing, and the History CSV can't be read by Excel as formulas.
