US spelling in the app: *Favorites* and *Accent color*. No other changes from 3.2.4.

**Already on 3.0 or later?** The app offers this update by itself within a day, or now from **Settings → Updates → Check for updates**. Installed with Scoop: `scoop update unifistockwatcher`.

**New here?** Download [`UnifiStockWatcher-Setup.exe`](https://github.com/jamesccupps/UnifiStockWatcher/releases/download/v3.2.5/UnifiStockWatcher-Setup.exe) (installs for your Windows user, no admin prompt) or the portable [`UnifiStockWatcher-win64.zip`](https://github.com/jamesccupps/UnifiStockWatcher/releases/download/v3.2.5/UnifiStockWatcher-win64.zip), or install it with [Scoop](https://scoop.sh):

```
scoop bucket add jamesccupps https://github.com/jamesccupps/scoop-bucket
scoop install jamesccupps/unifistockwatcher
```

If Windows shows *Windows protected your PC*, **More info** names the publisher, James Cupps; `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists every file's hash.

### Changed

- **US spelling**: the watch list's *Favorites*, the ★ button's *Add to favorites*, the *Only for favorites* switches and the *Accent color* setting. Only the words changed: your favorites, settings and exported watch lists carry over as they are, and the JSON that phone alerts send to an address of your own still has its `favourite` field, so automations reading it keep working.
