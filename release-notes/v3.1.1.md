A fix for settings and watch lists edited by hand. Everything from [3.1.0](https://github.com/jamesccupps/UnifiStockWatcher/releases/tag/v3.1.0), including alerts on your phone, is here too.

**Already on 3.0 or 3.1?** The app offers this update by itself within a day, or now from **Settings → Updates → Check for updates**.

**New here?** Download [`UnifiStockWatcher-Setup.exe`](https://github.com/jamesccupps/UnifiStockWatcher/releases/download/v3.1.1/UnifiStockWatcher-Setup.exe) (installs for your Windows user, no admin prompt) or the portable [`UnifiStockWatcher-win64.zip`](https://github.com/jamesccupps/UnifiStockWatcher/releases/download/v3.1.1/UnifiStockWatcher-win64.zip). The app isn't code-signed, so Windows may say *Windows protected your PC*: **More info → Run anyway**.

### Fixed

- A `settings.json` or watch list saved by an editor that starts the file with a byte-order mark (Windows PowerShell, some versions of Notepad and other editors) was treated as unreadable. The app started with default settings or an empty watch list, and the next change saved over the file. Such files now load normally, and importing one works.
