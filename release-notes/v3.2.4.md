
### Security

- **Code-signed** (Azure Artifact Signing, timestamped): `UnifiStockWatcher.exe` and `UnifiStockWatcher-cli.exe` in both downloads, `UnifiStockWatcher-Setup.exe` and the uninstaller. Windows shows the publisher in the file's Properties → Digital Signatures and in its prompts.
